Herding Cats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Herding Cats ("the game") is made by QuantumGlitch Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Android (package com.quantumglitch.herdingcats) or on iPhone and iPad.

The short version

The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your progress stays on your device. The full game is unlocked with one in-app purchase, paid through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details, and the game sends nothing to us.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • Snapshots. If you save a snapshot from the Cat Diary, the picture is saved on your device. It stays there unless you share it yourself.
  • The purchase. After you buy the full game, the game keeps a note on your device that it is unlocked.

In-app purchase

One optional purchase unlocks the full game. Payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. To make or restore the purchase, the game talks to the store, which needs an internet connection. The game does not use the internet for anything else. The store only tells the game that the purchase was made, so it can unlock. The game keeps that on your device and sends nothing to us.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for permission to make the device vibrate, to use the internet and see whether the device is online (for the purchase), and to use Google Play's billing service. On iPhone and iPad the game asks for no permissions.

Google Play and the App Store

You get the game through Google Play, a service run by Google, or through the App Store, a service run by Apple. They may collect information about your device and how you use their stores, including your purchases, under their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers, Google or Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. These reports do not tell us who you are.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles your information (for example by adding ads), we will update this policy before that version comes out and change the date at the top of this page.
