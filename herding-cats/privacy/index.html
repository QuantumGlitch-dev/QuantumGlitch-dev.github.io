Herding Cats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Herding Cats ("the game") is made by QuantumGlitch Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Android (package com.quantumglitch.herdingcats).

The short version

The game does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • Snapshots. If you save a snapshot from the Cat Diary, the picture is saved on your device. It stays there unless you share it yourself.

Permissions

The game asks Android only for permission to make the device vibrate.

Google Play

You get the game through Google Play, a service run by Google. Google may collect information about your device and how you use Google Play under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data, Google may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. These reports do not tell us who you are.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds anything that handles data (for example optional ads or an in-app purchase), we will update this policy before that version comes out and change the date at the top of this page.
