Herding Cats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Herding Cats ("the game") is made by QuantumGlitch Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Android (package com.quantumglitch.herdingcats) or on iPhone and iPad.

The short version

The game has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and your progress stays on your device. The free version shows video ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some information about your device to show them (see Ads below). The in-app purchase is paid through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details, and the game sends nothing to us.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • Snapshots. If you save a snapshot from the Cat Diary, the picture is saved on your device. It stays there unless you share it yourself.
  • The purchase. After you buy the full game, the game keeps a note on your device that it is unlocked.
  • Your ad choices. Google's consent tool saves your answers on your device, so the game does not ask every time.

Ads

The free version shows video ads from Google AdMob: a short video after the morning comic, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a boost. There are no banners. After the purchase the game shows no ads unless you choose to watch an optional video.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects your IP address (which can show your approximate location), how you use the game and the ads (for example app launches, taps and video views), diagnostics (for example launch time, hangs and battery use) and device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID on Android; on iPhone and iPad, identifiers limited to this app or to apps from the same developer, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. This information is sent to Google encrypted, and Google uses it under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. We only see summary reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for ads. You can change these choices at any time with "Ad privacy choices" in the game's settings. On iPhone and iPad the game asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you don't, your advertising identifier is not used. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

In-app purchase

One optional purchase unlocks the full game and removes the forced ads. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. The store only tells the game that the purchase was made, so it can unlock. The game keeps that on your device and sends nothing to us.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for permission to make the device vibrate, to use the internet and see whether the device is online (for ads and the purchase), to use Google Play's billing service and to use the advertising ID for ads. On iPhone and iPad it only asks whether you allow tracking for ads (see Ads).

Google Play and the App Store

You get the game through Google Play, a service run by Google, or through the App Store, a service run by Apple. They may collect information about your device and how you use their stores, including your purchases, under their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers, Google or Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. These reports do not tell us who you are.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The ads in the game are limited to content suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles your information, we will update this policy before that version comes out and change the date at the top of this page.
