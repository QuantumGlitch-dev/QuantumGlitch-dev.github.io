Grains of Salt

George Vale

The town is perfect. The children are happy. And the mountain is hungry.

Dan and Emma came to Norway for one last trip before starting a family. Solværd was only meant to be a detour: a postcard village famous for its hand-harvested salt and a festival it has kept for eight hundred years. The festival is in two days, and visitors are asked to leave before it begins.
